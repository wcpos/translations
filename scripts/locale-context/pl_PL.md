## Polish (pl_PL) — Locale-Specific Rules

- Address the user in the second person singular ("Wybierz", "Twój"), as pl_PL already ships; never "Pan" or "Pani".
- Required terminology (from glossary): Paragon (Receipt), Kasjer (Cashier), Zwrot / Zwróć (Refund), Bramka płatności (Payment gateway), W promocji (On sale; "W sprzedaży" means for sale), Szuflada (cash drawer).
- Plurals: `few` (2–4) is the nominative plural and `many` (5 and up) the genitive plural: {count} wpisy, {count} wpisów. They must differ when a noun follows the count, and neither drops the noun.
- Keep one noun across all plural forms of a key.
- "Stan" is never Stock: Stock is "Magazyn".
- Void is "Unieważnij", distinct from Cancel "Anuluj".
- Short forms (packet `short_forms`): Il. (Qty), Min. / Maks., Ponów (Retry), Eko (Eco); keep ID, FAQ, Sync and OK.
- Decimal separator is comma: 0,25 not 0.25.
- Use „ and ” for quotation marks, not straight quotes.
