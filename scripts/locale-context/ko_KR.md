## Korean (ko_KR) — Locale-Specific Rules

- Use polite conversational register (해요체) — NOT stiff formal (하십시오체). This is a POS application used by retail staff.
- 원하시나요 (correct) not 원하십니까 (too formal). 확인하세요 (correct) not 확인하십시오 (too formal).
- Required terminology: 영수증 (Receipt), 계산대 (Checkout), 캐셔 (Cashier), 결제 수단 (Gateway), 템플릿 (Template).
- Prefer native Korean over English loanwords where natural: 작업 over 액션, 용지 over 지면.
- Use proper Korean particles: 템플릿이 삭제되었습니다 (complete sentence with subject particle), not just 템플릿 삭제됨.
