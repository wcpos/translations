## Dutch (nl_NL) — Locale-Specific Rules

- Address the user informally with "je" and "jouw", as most nl_NL strings already do; do not mix in "u" or "uw".
- Buttons use the infinitive: "Verwijderen", "Verbinden", "Selecteren", not "Verwijder".
- Required terminology (from glossary): Bon (Receipt; Receipts is "Bonnen"), Kassa (Checkout at the till), Kassamedewerker (Cashier).
- Void is "Storneren", distinct from Cancel "Annuleren".
- Pending is "In afwachting"; "In behandeling" is Processing.
- A printer vendor is the "Fabrikant", not the "Leverancier" (supplier).
- Short forms (packet `short_forms`): Aant. (Qty), Min. / Max., Opnieuw (Retry); keep ID, FAQ, Sync, OK and Eco.
- Decimal separator is comma: 0,25 not 0.25.
- Use ‘ and ’ for quotation marks, not straight quotes.
