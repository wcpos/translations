## Portuguese, Brazil (pt_BR) — Locale-Specific Rules

- Address the user as "você" (seu, sua), as pt_BR already ships; never "tu" or "o senhor".
- Use sentence case, not English Title Case: "Data de pagamento", "Nome de usuário", "Em breve".
- Required terminology (from glossary): Recibo (Receipt), Caixa (Checkout at the till), Operador (Cashier), Sistema de pagamento (Payment gateway, as in WooCommerce).
- "Caixa" is only the register or the checkout, never the person: Cashier is "Operador".
- Receipts is "Recibos", not "Cupons": Coupons are "Cupons". Trash is "Lixeira", not "Lixo".
- Short forms (packet `short_forms`): Qtd. (Qty), Mín. / Máx., Repetir (Retry); keep ID, FAQ, Sync, OK and Eco.
- Decimal separator is comma: 0,25 not 0.25.
- Use “ and ” for quotation marks, not straight quotes.
