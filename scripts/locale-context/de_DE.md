## German (de_DE) — Locale-Specific Rules

- Use formal "Sie" throughout — this is business software.
- Prefer German terms (e.g. "Kassierer", not "Cashier"), but keep loanwords that German WooCommerce admins actually use and that WooCommerce's own German translation uses: "Barcode", "Upgrade", "Template".
- Required terminology (from glossary): Kassierer (Cashier), Beleg (Receipt), Kasse (Checkout at the till), Barcode, Upgrade, Zahlungsschnittstelle (Payment gateway, as in WooCommerce), Template, Erstattung (Refund, as in WooCommerce; "Rückerstattung" is also fine).
- Compound nouns are one word: "Rückerstattung" not "Rück Erstattung".
- Decimal separator is comma: 0,25 not 0.25.
- Use „ and " for quotation marks, not English quotes.
