## German (de_DE) — Locale-Specific Rules

- Use formal "Sie" throughout — this is business software.
- ALWAYS translate English words into German. German has native terms for nearly everything in this domain. Do NOT leave English words like "Cashier", "Upgrade", "Barcode", "Template" untranslated.
- Required terminology (from glossary): Kassierer (Cashier), Beleg (Receipt), Kasse (Checkout), Strichcode (Barcode), Aktualisierung (Upgrade), Zahlungsmethode (Gateway), Vorlage (Template), Rückerstattung (Refund).
- Compound nouns are one word: "Rückerstattung" not "Rück Erstattung".
- Decimal separator is comma: 0,25 not 0.25.
- Use „ and " for quotation marks, not English quotes.
