## Farsi/Persian (fa_IR) — Locale-Specific Rules

- RTL language. Placeholders and brand names stay LTR.
- Required terminology: رسید (Receipt — NEVER سیاره which means "planet"), الگو (Template), چاپگر رسید (Receipt printer).
- CRITICAL: "receipt" = رسید. The word سیاره means "planet" and must NEVER appear in a POS context.
- Use "الگو" for template, not "قالب".
- Format: noun+modifier order for borrowed terms: "کد QR" not "QR Code".
