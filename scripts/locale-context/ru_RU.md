## Russian (ru_RU) — Locale-Specific Rules

- Address the user formally with "вы" and polite imperatives ("Выберите", "Нажмите"), as ru_RU already ships; never "ты".
- Required terminology (from glossary): Чек (Receipt), Кассир (Cashier), Со скидкой (On sale; "В продаже" means for sale).
- Plurals: `few` (2–4) and `many` (5 and up) take different noun forms and must differ when a noun follows the count: 3 записи, 5 записей; 3 сеанса, 5 сеансов. Write each form as the grammar requires: never copy `few` into `many` by habit, but identical forms are correct where the grammar makes them so (the count comes last, as in "Принято платежей: {count}").
- Keep one noun across all plural forms of a key: сеанс / сеанса / сеансов, not сеанс / сессии.
- "Право" means law; right alignment is "Справа".
- Short forms (packet `short_forms`): Кол. (Qty), Мин. / Макс., Повторить (Retry), Эко (Eco); keep ID, FAQ, Sync and OK in Latin letters.
- Decimal separator is comma: 0,25 not 0.25.
- Use « and » for quotation marks, not straight quotes.
