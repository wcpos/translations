## French (fr_FR) — Locale-Specific Rules

- Use formal "vous" throughout.
- Decimal separator is COMMA: write 0,25 — never 0.25.
- Use "extension" for WordPress plugins (standard WordPress French), not "plugin".
- Use "requête" for API/server requests, not "demande" (which is vague).
- Required terminology: Reçu (Receipt), Caisse (Checkout), Caissier (Cashier), Passerelle de paiement (Gateway), Modèle (Template), Code-barres (Barcode), Remboursement (Refund).
- Use French abbreviation "p. ex." or "par ex." — never "eg:" or "e.g.".
- Grammatical note: after "et" (and), use an infinitive verb or a noun with article — NOT a bare noun. "Enregistrer et prévisualiser" (correct) not "Enregistrer et aperçu" (wrong).
