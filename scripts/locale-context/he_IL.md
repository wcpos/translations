## Hebrew (he_IL) — Locale-Specific Rules

- RTL language. Placeholders and brand names (POS, WooCommerce) stay LTR within RTL text.
- Use Hebrew gershayim (״) for abbreviations like דוא״ל — NOT escaped ASCII quotes.
- Prefer native Hebrew vocabulary over transliterations. "ניתוחים" (analyses) is better than "אנליטיקה" (transliteration of analytics).
- Required terminology: קבלה (Receipt), קופה (Checkout), שיטת תשלום (Payment gateway, as in WooCommerce's Hebrew translation).
- Translate ALL English text. Never leave English strings or fragments untranslated.
